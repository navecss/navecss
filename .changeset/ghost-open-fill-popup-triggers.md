A `ghost` button now keeps its fill while open only when what it opens is a menu, a popover or a dialog. The fill was keyed on `aria-expanded="true"` alone, so a ghost button used as the trigger of a `Collapsible`, an `Accordion`, a `NavigationMenu` or a `Select` held the same fill for as long as it was open, though the fill was only meant to mark an open menu, popover or dialog. On a `Collapsible` or `Accordion` trigger it reads as a pressed button, and while the section stays open a keyboard user can move focus to a neighbouring control, whose focus ring then lands on the held fill wherever the two sit closer together than the ring reaches; with the default tokens, in the dark scheme, that ring is below 3:1 against the fill. The key now also requires `aria-haspopup` to be `menu`, `true` or `dialog`, which Base UI sets on the `Menu`, `Popover`, `Dialog` and `Alert Dialog` triggers (including the `Menu.Trigger` in a Menubar), so those keep the fill. The other triggers show none while open, and still fill under the pointer and while pressed.
